Draws an arrow pointing at something, with a note at the end.

## How to use
1. Start **Leader** (`LE`) and click where the arrow points.
2. Click one or more bend points; press **Enter** after the last.
3. Type the note and press **Enter**.

## Tips
- Arrow and text size come from the [dimension style](help:dimstyle).
- Double-click the leader to change its note.
