Turns solids into **hole shapes**: see-through grey shapes that cut away material when you [Combine](help:combine) them with solids. Until then they change nothing, so you can move and resize them freely.

## How to use
1. Select the solids (or start **Make Hole** and pick them), press Enter.

## Tips
- Hole shapes aren't exported, measured or drawn in drawing views.
- [Make Solid](help:makesolid) turns them back.
