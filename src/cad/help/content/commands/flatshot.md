Makes plain 2D lines of the selected solids' visible edges seen from above, in the plan.

## How to use
1. Start **Flatshot**, select solids, press Enter.

## Tips
- For proper drawings with front/top/side views and hidden lines, use [Base View](help:viewbase) instead.
