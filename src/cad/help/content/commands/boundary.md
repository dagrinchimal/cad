Makes a closed polyline around an enclosed area: click inside, and the program traces the edges for you.

## How to use
1. Start **Boundary**.
2. Click inside the area.

## Tips
- The new polyline lies on top of the existing lines. Use it to [offset](help:offset) the inside of a room, or select it to read its area in the Properties panel.
