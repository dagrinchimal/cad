Moves solids by an x, y, z distance, including up and down.

## How to use
1. Start **3D Move**, select the solids, press Enter.
2. Type the displacement as `x,y,z`, e.g. `0,0,25` to lift by 25.

## Tips
- [Move](help:move) also moves solids, but only sideways in the plan.
