Changes how solids look in the 3D view.

## Styles
- **Wireframe** (`W`): edges only, see-through.
- **Hidden** (`H`): white faces, edges behind are hidden. Like a line drawing.
- **Shaded** (`S`): colored, lit faces.
- **Shaded with edges** (`E`): shaded plus edges (the default).
- **X-ray** (`X`): see-through shaded faces with all edges.
