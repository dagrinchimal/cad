Turns solids around the X, Y or Z axis through a point, e.g. to stand a part on its side.

## How to use
1. Start **3D Rotate**, select the solids, press Enter.
2. Choose the axis: `X`, `Y` or `Z`.
3. Click or type a point the axis passes through (`x,y,z` for a height).
4. Type the angle in degrees.
