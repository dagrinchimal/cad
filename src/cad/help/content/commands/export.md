Saves a copy of the drawing in another format: **DXF**, **DWG**, **PDF**, **SVG**, **PNG**, **JPEG**, **TIFF** or **HP-GL/2**.

## How to use
1. Start **Export** (`EXP`).
2. Pick the **Format** and the **File** name.
3. Set the options. For paper formats: what to export (model, a layout, or all), paper size, scale, colors, lineweights; for images, the resolution.
4. Read the note at the bottom: it lists what this format can't keep from your drawing.
5. Click **Export**.

## Tips
- Exporting several sheets to SVG or an image makes one file per sheet; PDF puts them all in one file.
- Your choices are remembered per format.
