Draws a circle.

## How to use
1. Start **Circle** and click the center.
2. Move the mouse and click, or type the radius (e.g. `25`).

## Options
- **Diameter** (`D`): after the center, type the diameter instead of the radius.
- **2P** (`2P`): pick two points on opposite sides (they become the diameter).
- **3P** (`3P`): pick any three points on the circle.

## Tips
- The ribbon's Circle button has a small arrow with the 2-Point and 3-Point versions.
- Snap to a circle's center with the **Center** object snap.
- Click a circle to see its radius in blue; click the number to type a new one.
