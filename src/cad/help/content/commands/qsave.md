Saves the drawing to its file. A new drawing asks for a name first.

## How to use
Press **Ctrl+S**.

## Tips
- A drawing opened from DXF or DWG is saved back as DXF or DWG. The first time, you're told what those formats can't keep; save a `.cadz` copy to keep everything.
- The program also saves a safety copy every few minutes ([Recover](help:recover)).
