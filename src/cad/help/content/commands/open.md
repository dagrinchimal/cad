Opens a drawing: our own `.cadz` files, or AutoCAD **DXF** and **DWG** and **SVG** files.

## How to use
Press **Ctrl+O**, choose the file, click **Open**. You can also drag a file from Explorer onto the window.

## Tips
- An opened DXF or DWG saves back to the same file when you press Ctrl+S.
- DWG needs the free ODA File Converter; see [File formats](help:file-formats).
- To add another file's contents into the drawing you have open, use [Import](help:import) (or hold **Ctrl** while dropping the file).
