On a layout sheet, draws a window that shows the model. Set its scale (1:50, 1/4" = 1'-0"…) to print the drawing to scale.

## How to use
1. Switch to a layout tab at the bottom of the window.
2. Start **Viewport** (`MV`) and click two corners.
3. Select the viewport's edge and choose a **Scale** in the Properties panel (it starts as "scale to fit"). Change **Model center X/Y** there to choose what part of the model shows.

## Tips
- Put viewport frames on a layer you turn off or set to not plot; new layouts already use a non-printing "Viewports" layer.
