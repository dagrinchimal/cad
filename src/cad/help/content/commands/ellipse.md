Draws an ellipse (oval), or part of one.

## How to use
1. Start **Ellipse** and click one end of an axis.
2. Click the other end of that axis.
3. Click or type the distance to the other axis (half its length).

## Options
- **Center** (`C`): click the center first, then an axis end, then the other axis distance.
- **Arc** (`A`): draw an elliptical arc; after the ellipse, give the start and end angles.

## Tips
- A circle stretched or scaled unevenly becomes an ellipse automatically.
