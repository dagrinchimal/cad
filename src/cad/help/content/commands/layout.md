Creates, renames or deletes layout sheets. A new layout comes with a border, a title block and a viewport showing the whole model.

## How to use
1. Start **Layout** (`LO`).
2. Choose **New** (`N`), **Rename** (`R`) or **Delete** (`D`), then type the name(s).

## Tips
- The **+** tab next to the layout tabs makes a new layout too; right-click a tab to rename or delete it.
- Double-click the title block to fill in its fields (title, date, scale…).
