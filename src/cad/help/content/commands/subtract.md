Cuts solids out of other solids: holes, pockets, notches, slots.

## How to use
1. Start **Subtract**.
2. Select the solids to keep (to cut FROM), press Enter.
3. Select the solids to take away, press Enter. They're used up.

## Tips
- [Extrude](help:extrude) › **Cut** does extrude-and-subtract in one step, and **Through all** makes through holes.
