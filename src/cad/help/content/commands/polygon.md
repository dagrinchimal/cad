Draws a regular polygon (all sides equal), such as a triangle, hexagon or octagon, as a closed polyline.

## How to use
1. Start **Polygon** and type the number of sides (e.g. `6`).
2. Click the center.
3. Choose **Inscribed** (corners touch the circle) or **Circumscribed** (sides touch the circle).
4. Click or type the radius.

## Options
- **Edge** (`E`): instead of a center, click the two ends of one side.

## Tips
- For a hex nut across flats, use **Circumscribed** with half the across-flats size.
