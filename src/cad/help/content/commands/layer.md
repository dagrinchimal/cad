Creates and manages layers. Each layer has a color, linetype and lineweight that its objects use (when their own are set to ByLayer), and can be turned off, frozen, locked or excluded from printing.

## Columns
- **✓**: the current layer (new objects go on it). Double-click to make a layer current.
- **Name**: double-click to rename.
- **On**: unticked hides the layer.
- **Freeze**: hides it and skips it entirely (faster on big drawings).
- **Lock**: you can see and snap to its objects but not change them.
- **Color**, **Linetype**, **Lineweight**: the layer's defaults.
- **Plot**: unticked means the layer shows on screen but never prints or exports to paper formats.
- **Description**: a note.

## Buttons
**New layer**, **Delete layer** (only empty layers; not layer 0), **Set current**.

## Tips
- The layer box on the Home tab switches the current layer quickly.
