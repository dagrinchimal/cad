Places small point markers that you can snap to with the **Node** snap.

## How to use
1. Start **Point**.
2. Click or type each point. Keep clicking to place more.
3. Press **Enter** when done.

## Tips
- Points are handy as reference marks, e.g. a surveyed corner. [ID](help:id) tells you a point's coordinates.
- Points print as a tiny dot.
