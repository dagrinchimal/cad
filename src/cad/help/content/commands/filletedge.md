Rounds edges of a solid.

## How to use
1. Start **Fillet Edge**. Type `R` to change the radius first if needed.
2. Click the edges to round (in the [3D view](help:view3d) is easiest). Click an edge again to un-pick it.
3. Press Enter.

## Tips
- In the plan you can pick edges seen from above; the 3D view lets you pick any edge.
- If it fails, try a smaller radius: it can't be bigger than the faces next to the edge.
