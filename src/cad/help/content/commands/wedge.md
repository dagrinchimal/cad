A box sloping from full height at the first corner's side down to nothing.

## How to use
1. Click or type the first corner.
2. Click or type the other corner.
3. Type the height. The tall end is at the low-X side.

## Tips
- Combine shapes with [Union](help:union), [Subtract](help:subtract) and [Intersect](help:intersect).
- In the plan you see the solid from above; type `VIEW3D` to see it in 3D.
