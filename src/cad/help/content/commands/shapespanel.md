---
id: shapespanel
type: command
title: Shapes Panel
command: SHAPESPANEL
synonyms: [shape library, basic shapes, parts bin]
related: [dropshape, makehole, combine]
glossary: [hole shape]
---
Shows or hides the **Shapes** panel: boxes, cylinders, spheres, cones, rings and wedges, as solids and as grey hole shapes. Drag one into the 3D view, or click it and then click where it goes. The panel opens by itself the first time you go into the 3D view.
