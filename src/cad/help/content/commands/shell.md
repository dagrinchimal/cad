Hollows out a solid, leaving walls of a set thickness. The faces you pick are left open.

## How to use
1. Start **Shell**.
2. Click the faces to remove (e.g. the top of a box to make an open tray). Press Enter. Press Enter straight away for a closed hollow solid (you then select the solid).
3. Type the wall thickness.

## Tips
- Walls grow inward, so the outside size stays the same.
