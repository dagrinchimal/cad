Shows or hides the **Properties** panel on the right. It lists everything about the selected objects (layer, color, linetype, lineweight, sizes, area, text) and lets you change it.

## How to use
- Press **Ctrl+1**, or type `PR`.
- Select objects; change a value and press **Enter**. With several objects selected, layer, color, linetype and lineweight changes apply to all of them.
- With nothing selected, the panel sets the layer, color, linetype and lineweight for **new** objects.
