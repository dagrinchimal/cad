Draws part of a circle.

## How to use (3 points)
1. Start **Arc** and click the start point.
2. Click a point the arc passes through.
3. Click the end point.

## Options
- **Center** (`C`): click the center first, then the start point, then where it should end. The arc runs counter-clockwise from the start, like AutoCAD.

## Tips
- A door swing: **Arc** › **Center**, click the hinge, the open-door end, then the closed-door end.
- To round off a corner between two lines, [Fillet](help:fillet) is quicker.
