Copies objects around a center point, like bolt holes or chairs around a table.

## How to use
1. Select the objects and start **Polar Array**.
2. Click the center point.
3. Type the number of items (including the original).
4. Type the angle to fill (`360` for a full circle).
5. Answer whether the copies turn as they go around (**Yes**) or keep their direction (**No**).

## Tips
- Like the rectangular array, it stays live; edit it in the Properties panel.
