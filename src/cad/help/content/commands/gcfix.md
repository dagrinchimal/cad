Locks a point (or a whole object) in place so other constraints move everything else instead.

## How to use
Start **Fix** and click near the point to lock.

## Options
- **Object** (`O`): lock a whole object.

## Tips
- Fixing one corner of a sketch is a good first step: it stops the whole shape from drifting when you change a size.
