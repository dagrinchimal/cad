Dimensions an angle.

## How to use
1. Start **Angular Dimension** (`DAN`).
2. Click the first line, then the second line. Or click an arc to dimension the angle it spans.
   Or press **Enter** and click the vertex, then a point on each side.
3. Click where the dimension arc goes; the side you click chooses which angle is shown.

## Tips
- Select one of the lines and click the angle's number to type a new angle: the line you selected turns.
