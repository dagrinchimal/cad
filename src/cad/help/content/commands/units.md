Chooses how lengths are shown and what a bare number means: millimeters, fractional inches, or architectural feet-and-inches. Also sets precision and how feet-inches are typed.

## Settings
- **Units**: Millimeters (`1250.50`), Fractional inches (`3 1/2"`), or Architectural (`3'-4 1/2"`).
- **Millimeter decimal places**, **Inch precision** (nearest 1/2" … 1/64"), **Angle decimal places**.
- **Typing feet-inches**: *Revit style* (`3 4-1/2` = 3'-4 1/2", `0 1.5` = 1 1/2", a bare `12` = 12 feet) or *AutoCAD style* (a bare `12` = 12 inches).

## Tips
- Changing units never changes the size of anything: it only changes how numbers are shown and read.
- You can always type any unit: `25.4mm`, `1"`, `2'` work in every drawing.
- The units box at the bottom right of the window switches the display quickly.
