Cuts solids in two with a flat plane.

## How to use
1. Start **Slice**, select the solids, press Enter.
2. Pick two points of a cutting line in the plan (the plane is vertical through them), or type `XY`, `YZ` or `ZX` and give a point the plane passes through (type `x,y,z` for a height).
3. Choose what to keep: **Both** (the default), **First side** or **Other side**.
