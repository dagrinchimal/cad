Breaks a compound object into simple ones: a polyline into lines and arcs, a block into its parts, a dimension into lines and text, a hatch into lines, an array into separate copies.

## How to use
Select the objects and start **Explode** (shortcut `X`).

## Tips
- Exploded dimensions and hatches no longer follow their objects. Usually you don't need to explode them.
