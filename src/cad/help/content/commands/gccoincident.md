Makes two points meet and stay together, or keeps a point on a line or circle.

## How to use
1. Start **Coincident** and click near the end (or center) of the first object: the nearest point is used.
2. Click near the point on the second object.

## Options
- **Object** (`O`): instead of a second point, click a line or circle the first point must stay on.
