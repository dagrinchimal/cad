Makes a parallel copy of a line, arc, circle or polyline at a set distance: wall thicknesses, borders, outlines.

## How to use
1. Start **Offset** and type the distance (e.g. `6"` or `150`).
2. Click the object to offset.
3. Click on the side where the copy goes.
4. Repeat steps 2–3 for more objects; press **Enter** when done.

## Options
- **Through** (`T`): instead of a distance, click a point the copy should pass through.
- **Exit** (`E`): finish.

## Tips
- The distance is remembered for next time; just press **Enter** to reuse it.
- Offsetting a closed polyline makes a bigger or smaller copy of the whole outline.
