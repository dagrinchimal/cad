Joins the selected solids into one and cuts away every hole shape among them, in one step. This is Tinkercad's **Group**.

## How to use
1. Select the solids and hole shapes together (click them, or a window around them), then start **Combine**. Or start Combine and pick them, then press Enter.

## Tips
- Only holes: they're joined into one bigger hole shape.
- The result takes the color and layer of the first solid.
- The same as [Union](help:union) of the solids then [Subtract](help:subtract) of the holes.
