Makes copies of objects at new places. Keeps placing copies until you press Enter.

## How to use
1. Select the objects and press **Enter** (or select first, then start **Copy**).
2. Click a base point.
3. Click or type each place for a copy. Press **Enter** or choose **Exit** when done.

## Tips
- For evenly spaced rows and columns use [Array](help:arrayrect); around a center use [Polar Array](help:arraypolar).
- Copies of objects with dimensions don't bring the dimensions unless you select them too.
