Sets up the drawing aids: grid, snap, ortho, polar tracking and object snaps.

## Settings
- **Show grid** (F7) and **Grid spacing**; **Snap to grid** (F9) and **Snap spacing**.
- **Ortho** (F8): only horizontal and vertical. Holding **Shift** while drawing flips it temporarily.
- **Polar tracking** (F10) and its **Increment angle**: the cursor locks onto those angles with a dotted guide.
- **Object snap** (F3): tick which points to lock onto: endpoint, midpoint, center, intersection, perpendicular, tangent, nearest, quadrant, node, extension, insertion.
- **Object snap tracking** (F11): line up with snap points you've hovered over.
- **Snap reach**: how close (in pixels) the cursor must be to grab a point.

See [Snaps and tracking](help:snaps).
