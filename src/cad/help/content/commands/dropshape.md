Puts a ready-made shape (box, cylinder, sphere, cone, torus, wedge) where you click, 20 mm across (1" in inch drawings). Then stretch it with its handles. The quickest way to start a 3D part.

## How to use
- **With the mouse:** drag a shape from the [Shapes panel](help:shapespanel) into the 3D view. It lands on whatever is under the cursor: the ground or the top of another solid. Or click a shape in the panel, then click where it goes.
- **Typed:** start **Add Shape**, choose the shape, then click or type where it goes (`x,y,z`). Keep clicking to add more; press Enter when done.

## Options
- **Hole** (`H`): make a hole shape instead (see-through grey; it cuts what you [Combine](help:combine) it with).
- **Size** (`S`): type the size before placing: `80,40,30` (width, depth, height) or one number for all three.

## After placing
The new shape is selected, so in the 3D view its **handles** show:
- square handles on the sides stretch it one way (the opposite side stays put);
- the blue handle on top makes it taller;
- the white cone above lifts it off the ground;
- drag the shape itself to slide it around;
- click a size number to type an exact size.
