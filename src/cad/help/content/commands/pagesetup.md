Chooses the paper size and orientation of the current layout.

## How to use
1. Open the layout's tab, then start **Page Setup**.
2. Pick a **Paper size** (ISO A4–A0, Letter, Tabloid, ANSI C/D, ARCH D/E) and tick **Portrait** for a tall sheet.

## Tips
- The title block keeps its size. For a matching border on a new paper size, create a new layout.
