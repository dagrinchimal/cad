Changes how close you are to the drawing. Usually you just turn the mouse wheel (it zooms around the cursor).

## How to use
Start **Zoom** (`Z`), then drag a window, or choose an option.

## Options
- **Extents** (`E`): fit everything ([Zoom Extents](help:zoomextents)). Double-clicking the middle mouse button does the same.
- **Previous** (`P`): back to the last view.
- **In** (`I`) / **Out** (`O`): twice as close / twice as far.
