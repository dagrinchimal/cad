Shows each selected solid's volume, surface area, center of mass and overall size.

## How to use
1. Start **Mass Properties**, select solids (or type `ALL`), press Enter.
2. Read the results in the command history.

## Tips
- For weight, multiply the volume by the material's density: steel is about 7.85 g/cm³ (0.00785 g/mm³), aluminium about 2.7 g/cm³.
