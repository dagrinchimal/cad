Opens this help. Press **F1** at any time:
- during a command, you get that command's page;
- with the mouse over a ribbon button, you get that tool's page;
- otherwise, the help home page.

## Finding things
Type in the search box at the top: a command name (`TRIM`), part of one, or what you want to do in your own words ("copy in a circle", "make a wall thicker", "print to scale"). Small typos are fine. Press **Enter** to open the best match.

On a command's page, **▶ Start** runs the command in the drawing.

## Keys
**Alt+Left** / **Alt+Right**: back and forward · **Ctrl+F**: search · **Esc**: close.
