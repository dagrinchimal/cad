A ring (donut) from a center, the ring radius and the tube radius.

## How to use
1. Click or type the center.
2. Type the radius of the ring (center to the middle of the tube).
3. Type the radius of the tube.

## Tips
- Combine shapes with [Union](help:union), [Subtract](help:subtract) and [Intersect](help:intersect).
- In the plan you see the solid from above; type `VIEW3D` to see it in 3D.
