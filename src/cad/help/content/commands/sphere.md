A sphere from a center and a radius.

## How to use
1. Click or type the center (`x,y,z` to lift it).
2. Type the radius.

## Tips
- Combine shapes with [Union](help:union), [Subtract](help:subtract) and [Intersect](help:intersect).
- In the plan you see the solid from above; type `VIEW3D` to see it in 3D.
