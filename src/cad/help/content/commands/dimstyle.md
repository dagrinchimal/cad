Sets how dimensions and leaders look: text height, arrow size and type, gaps, decimal places and the overall scale.

## Settings
- **Text height**, **Arrow size**, **Gap from object**, **Extension past dimension line**, **Gap around text**, **Center mark size**: sizes as they should print.
- **Arrowheads**: closed filled arrow, open arrow, architectural tick, or dot.
- **Overall scale**: multiplies every size. Dimensioning in model space for a 1/4" = 1'-0" print, use 48; for 1:50, use 50. On a layout sheet, leave it at 1.
- **Decimal places**: or "Use drawing units" to follow the [Units](help:units) setting.

## Tips
- Existing dimensions update when you change the style.
