Draws a rectangle as one closed [polyline](help:pline).

## How to use
1. Start **Rectangle** and click one corner.
2. Click the opposite corner, or type it relative to the first one, e.g. `@120,80` (in feet-inch drawings: `@12',10'`).

## Options
- **Dimensions** (`D`): type the length and the width, then click which side to draw it on.

## Tips
- Click a rectangle and type a new length into one of its blue numbers: the far side moves and it stays square-cornered.
- [Offset](help:offset) a rectangle to make the inside face of a wall.
