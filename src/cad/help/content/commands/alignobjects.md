Lines objects up along X, Y or Z: their left, right, front, back, top or bottom sides, or their centers. Works on solids and 2D objects.

## How to use
1. Select the objects (at least two), start **Align**.
2. Choose the direction: `X` (left–right), `Y` (front–back) or `Z` (up–down).
3. Choose **Min** (`MI`: left/front/bottom), **Center** (`C`) or **Max** (`MA`: right/back/top). Everything moves to line up with the outermost one (or the middle of them all).

## Tips
- To center a hole on a block: select both, Align X Center, then Align Y Center.
