Turns objects around a point.

## How to use
1. Select the objects and press **Enter**.
2. Click the point to turn around.
3. Type the angle (counter-clockwise is positive, e.g. `90`, `-45`) or click.

## Options
- **Copy** (`C`): leaves the original in place and rotates a copy.
- **Reference** (`R`): give the angle the objects are at now, then the angle you want. Pick two points along an edge for the reference to line it up with something else.

## Tips
- Angles follow the drawing's angle settings in [Units](help:units).
