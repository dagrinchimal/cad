Removes the piece of an object between two points, e.g. to make an opening in a wall line.

## How to use
1. Start **Break** and click the object. Where you click is the first break point.
2. Click the second break point.

## Options
- **First point** (`F`): pick the first break point separately (useful when you selected the object somewhere else).
