Shows how far a point is from the origin (0,0) in X or Y, as used on machined parts where every hole is measured from one corner.

## How to use
1. Put the part's datum corner at 0,0 (move it there).
2. Start **Ordinate Dimension** (`DOR`) and click the feature (e.g. a hole center).
3. Move straight up or down to show the X distance, or sideways to show Y, and click.

## Options
- **Xdatum** (`X`) / **Ydatum** (`Y`): force which coordinate is shown.
