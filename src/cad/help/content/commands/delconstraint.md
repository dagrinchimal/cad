title: Delete Constraints
command: DELCONSTRAINT
synonyms: [remove constraints, unconstrain, free shape]
related: [constraints, constraintbar]
glossary: [geometric constraint, dimensional constraint]
---
Removes every constraint on the selected objects. The objects keep their current shape.

## How to use
Select the objects, start **Delete Constraints**, and press **Enter**.
