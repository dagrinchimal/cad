Moves objects by a distance and direction.

## How to use
1. Select the objects (before or after starting **Move**), then press **Enter**.
2. Click a **base point**, a spot you can name, such as a corner.
3. Click or type where that spot should go. `@500,0` moves 500 to the right; or point the mouse and type `500`.

## Options
- **Displacement** (`D`): type the move as x,y (e.g. `120,40`) without picking points.

## Tips
- Snap the base point to a corner and the destination to another object to place things exactly.
- Dimensions, hatches and arrays attached to what you move follow it.
