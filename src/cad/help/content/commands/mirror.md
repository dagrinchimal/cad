Makes a mirror image of objects across a line.

## How to use
1. Select the objects and press **Enter**.
2. Click two points on the mirror line.
3. Answer **No** to keep the originals, **Yes** to remove them.

## Tips
- Text stays readable: it's moved, not written backwards.
- Turn on Ortho (F8) for a straight horizontal or vertical mirror line.
