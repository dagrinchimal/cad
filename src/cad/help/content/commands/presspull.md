Click a flat face of a solid and push it in or pull it out.

## How to use
1. Start **Press/Pull**.
2. Click a flat face. In the plan, clicking a solid picks its top face; in the 3D view, any face you can see.
3. Type a distance: positive pulls the face outward, negative pushes it in.
