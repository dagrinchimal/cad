Click a flat face of a solid and push it in or pull it out.

## How to use
1. Start **Press/Pull**.
2. Click a flat face of a solid, **or click inside any closed shape you drew** (on the ground or on a face/sketch). In the plan, clicking picks what's on top; in the 3D view, whatever you can see.
3. In the 3D view, move the mouse: an orange arrow and a live preview follow it. Click to finish, or type the distance. Positive pulls outward, negative pushes in.

## Shapes you drew (SketchUp-style)
- A shape drawn on the ground pulled up becomes a new solid.
- A shape drawn on a solid's face pulled out adds to that solid; pushed in, it cuts a pocket or a hole.
- Draw on a face by starting a drawing tool in the 3D view and clicking on the face first.
