Layers are like transparent sheets stacked on top of each other: walls on one, furniture on another, dimensions on a third. Each layer has a color, linetype and lineweight, and can be hidden, locked or kept off prints.

- New objects go on the **current layer**: pick it in the layer box on the Home tab.
- Objects normally use their layer's color, linetype and lineweight (**ByLayer**). Change the layer's color and all its objects change.
- Move objects to another layer by selecting them and choosing the layer in the Properties panel.
- Manage layers in [Layer Properties](help:layer): on/off, freeze, lock, plot, color, linetype, lineweight.

Layer **0** always exists and can't be deleted. Draw block contents on layer 0 so inserted copies take the layer they're placed on.
